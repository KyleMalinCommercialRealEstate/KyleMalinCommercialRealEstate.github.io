Commercial Real Estate Investment Sales & Advisory

Strategic guidance for owners and investors.

A commercial real estate agent with Marcus & Millichap specializing in Industrial, Retail, Multifamily/Apartments, Mixed-Use, Specialty/Marina, and Medical Office and Traditional Office investment properties throughout Maryland, Pennsylvania, West Virginia, and Delaware. Malin advises investors and property owners on the acquisition and disposition of commercial real estate, helping clients maximize value and identify strategic opportunities across the Mid-Atlantic region, including 1031 exchange transactions and long-term investment strategies.

Since joining Marcus & Millichap in 2021, Malin has built a reputation for delivering client-focused advisory services while working to extract maximum value for sellers and identify the right investment opportunities for buyers. He prioritizes maintaining productive relationships with all parties throughout the transaction process while ensuring his clients achieve their investment objectives. In recognition of his performance, Malin was named Rookie of the Year in 2023 and Weekend Warrior in 2025 for the Baltimore and Washington, D.C. offices.

Originally from Hockessin, Delaware, Malin earned a degree in Civil Engineering from the University of Delaware and later completed his MBA at the University of Maryland’s Robert H. Smith School of Business. His technical and business background provides a strong analytical foundation that supports his work advising investors on Industrial, Retail, and Office investment real estate throughout the region.

Currently licensed in Maryland, Pennsylvania, Delaware, and West Virginia, with the ability to work in any area of the United States and its territories.
